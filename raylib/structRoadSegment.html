Detailed Description

A directed road segment connecting two RoadNode instances.

Stores lane count, speed limit and the pre-computed quadratic Bezier polyline used for rendering and agent navigation.

Generates the quadratic Bezier polyline between two nodes.

The control point is offset perpendicularly from the midpoint to produce an organic curve. The sign of the offset alternates based on node IDs.
